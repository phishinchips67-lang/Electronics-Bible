CHAPTER 1.2 · FROM ROCK TO RADIO

Not orbits.
Just charge.

Every circuit you'll ever build traces back to one unexplained fact: some particles push, some pull, and it has nothing to do with them spinning around anything.

CHARGE · FIELD · POTENTIAL · GROUND
01 · What Is Charge, Really?

It was never the orbiting. The charge was always there.

Here's the misconception worth killing first: charge is not something an electron generates by circling a proton. An electron sitting perfectly still, with no proton anywhere nearby, still has exactly the same charge it always had. Motion has nothing to do with it.

Frozen Still
⚛️

The electron's charge is intrinsic — it doesn't require motion to exist.

Charge is quantized. It comes in fixed-size steps, all whole-number multiples of one elementary unit, e ≈ 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
02 · Coulomb's Law

Same shape as gravity. Almost incomprehensibly stronger.

CHAPTER 1.3 · FROM ROCK TO RADIO

Every magnet is just
tiny electrons agreeing.

Magnetism isn't a separate force from electricity — it's the same force, seen from a different angle.

DOMAINS · FIELD LINES · LORENTZ FORCE · MOTORS
01 · Why Magnets Work at All

A magnet is billions of tiny compass needles, all pointing the same way.

Domain Alignment
⬆️⬆️⬆️⬆️⬆️

Every arrow is a magnetic domain. Aligned, they reinforce into one strong field.

Unmagnetized iron: domains point every which way and cancel out. Magnetized iron: domains snap into alignment and add up.
02 · The Lorentz Force

The one equation that explains every motor ever built.

F = qv × B

F = qv × B

A charged particle moving through a magnetic field feels a force perpendicular to both its velocity and the field. This steers it into a circle.

🌀
Right-hand rule: thumb = velocity, fingers = field, palm = force (for positive charges). Flip the charge, flip the direction.
END OF CHAPTER 1.3

Electricity and magnetism were never two separate things.
